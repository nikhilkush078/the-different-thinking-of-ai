# 🤖 How Does AI Think?


## 📌 About

This presentation explores how AI interprets human instructions and visual context through a series of image-editing experiments. Starting with simple prompts such as adding objects or removing people, the experiments gradually introduce ambiguous instructions where AI must decide what the user means.

The final experiment asks AI to **“remove the bad person”** from an image. While a human may interpret the situation based on the social context of the scene, the AI produces a different interpretation and removes another person. This demonstrates how AI can interpret the same visual situation differently from humans.

## 🧪 Experiments

* 🎨 Making an image colorful
* 🎂 Adding a birthday hat
* 👩 Removing one person
* 🐱 Removing one cat
* 👥 Removing any person
* ⚠️ Removing the “bad person”
* 🔍 Comparing human and AI interpretation

## 💡 Key Idea

The presentation does not claim that AI thinks exactly like humans. Instead, it explores how AI uses learned patterns, visual context, and relationships within an image to interpret ambiguous instructions—and how its interpretation can sometimes differ significantly from human expectations.

> **The interesting question is not only “Can AI understand our instructions?” but also “How does AI interpret them?”**
